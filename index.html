🔒 Private by design개인정보는 기기 안에만

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads.
  • Progress is saved only in this device's browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The report-card import is read on the device and the child's name is not saved.
  • 계정, 가입, 광고가 없어요.
  • 기록은 이 기기의 브라우저에만 저장되고 업로드하지 않아요.
